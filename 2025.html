Rotary Adventure in Citizenship

I participated in Rotary Adventure in Citizenship, a national youth program in Ottawa that brings students together to explore Canadian democracy, citizenship and public institutions. The experience offered an opportunity to exchange perspectives with young people from across Canada and develop a deeper understanding of civic participation. My involvement continued after the program: I run the 2025 alumni Instagram page (@aic2025alumni), maintaining a space for participants to stay connected.
