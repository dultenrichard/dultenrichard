Sources and accuracy

This personal website distinguishes firsthand accounts and privately held supporting records from independent news coverage and public competition results. A link to an organization is not an endorsement or independent verification of every biographical claim. Dates, titles and outcomes are published only as supported; uncertain details are identified.

Articles and corrections

Perspectives are personal reflections. Published perspectives are reviewed and approved by the named author, who takes responsibility for their content. No quotations, attributed opinions or claims of outcomes should be added without a source. To request a correction, contact me through the press page. Substantive corrections should be dated and described on the affected page.

Photographs

Images are supplied for this website and credited where authorship is known. See the provenance registry. Publication does not automatically grant third-party reuse rights. Private addresses, precise photo locations and contributor details are not intentionally published.

Privacy

The homepage and press page load Umami analytics to understand visits and site interactions. The existing configuration requests respect for Do Not Track. GitHub Pages hosting, Google Fonts and any external sites you choose to visit may process technical request information under their own policies. This site has no local contact form or user account system; interview booking and LinkedIn enquiries are handled by those external providers.

Analytics retention and provider account settings have not been independently verified. Do not send sensitive information through public channels. For privacy or correction questions, use the contact options on the press page.
