Short biography

Dulten Richard is a Canadian student, Air Cadet instructor and co-founder of Bring Back Cadets. His experience includes cadet leadership, Rotary Adventure in Citizenship and a speaking engagement at the Canadian War Museum in 2025. His interests include youth advocacy, public policy and entrepreneurship.
