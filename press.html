150-word biography

Dulten Richard is a Canadian student, Air Cadet instructor and co-founder of Bring Back Cadets, a campaign advocating for youth opportunities in the Canadian Cadet Program. He joined Air Cadets in 2021 and has held cadet leadership and instructional responsibilities, including mentoring cadets and delivering training. In 2025, he participated in Rotary Adventure in Citizenship, spoke at the Canadian War Museum and received mock trial recognition. In 2026, he served as a Flight Sergeant Instructor at Blackdown Cadet Training Centre, leading a platoon for two weeks and supporting instruction for four weeks. His cadet recognition includes the Lord Strathcona Medal, Air Cadet Service Medal and Top Cadet Instructor award. As part of Bring Back Cadets, he created a petition calling for improvements to sexual assault reporting procedures. He has played rugby since Grade 9 and competed in indoor rowing. His interests include public policy, international affairs, entrepreneurship and youth advocacy.
